Questions

What owners ask before they start.

Does it need the internet?

No. Selling never needs the internet. It is only used, when it is there, for GRA E-VAT, the owner's daily email and updates. Sales made offline are sent to GRA when the connection returns.

Will my barcode scanner and receipt printer work?

A standard USB barcode scanner works without any setup. Receipt printers and cash drawers are set up under Settings, with a test print. Tell us your models and we will confirm before you buy.

Can I have more than one till?

Yes. One computer is the main till and keeps the books; the other tills and kitchen screens connect to it over your shop's network and share one stock count and one receipt sequence.

What if the computer breaks or is stolen?

MoRaj POS backs itself up while you work, seals each backup with your passphrase, and can copy it to a flash drive or a synced folder. Restore it on the new computer and carry on.

Can my cashiers steal with it?

Every person signs in as themselves, a manager approves large discounts and refunds, and the drawer is counted before the expected figure is shown. Nothing hides a shortage.
