What protects the books

Designed for the day something goes wrong.

An accounting system earns its keep in the bad week, not the good one.

  • Nothing is edited awayA posted entry cannot be changed. A correction is a reversal that keeps the original and the correction on the record, which is what an auditor expects to see.
  • A second pair of eyesLarge payments and hand-keyed journals wait for a second approver, at thresholds you set.
  • It checks itselfAfter every posting it re-proves that each sub-ledger agrees with its control account, and says so the moment one does not.
  • Backups that are provenScheduled backups, encrypted copies safe to keep in a cloud folder, and a one-click drill that restores into a scratch copy to prove the backup works.
  • Months you can closeSign off a reconciled month and lock a finished year, so the figures you reported stay the figures in the books.
  • A full audit trailEvery person signs in as themselves, and every action is recorded against their name.

Questions

What people ask before they start.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything runs on your computer. The internet is only used, when it is there, to check for a newer version and to fetch Bank of Ghana exchange rates, which you can also type in yourself.

Where are my books kept?

On your own computer, in a standard PostgreSQL database in the application's folder. Backups go to a folder you choose, and can be encrypted with a passphrase so they are safe to keep in a cloud folder.

What if the computer is lost or breaks?

Restore your latest backup on the new machine and carry on. The one-click restore drill lets you prove a backup works before you ever need it, without touching your live books.

Can more than one person use it?

Yes. Each person gets their own sign-in and role on the licensed computer, larger payments can require a second approver, and every action is recorded against the person who took it.

We use Excel or another package today. Can we move across?

Yes. You start from your opening balances and your chart of accounts. For founding customers we do that setup with you, along with your first bank reconciliation.

What happens if we stop paying for updates?

Nothing stops. The licence is yours for ever, and the version you have keeps working. Updates are optional, at 20% of the licence a year.
