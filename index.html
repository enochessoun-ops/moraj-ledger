What protects the books

Designed for the day something goes wrong.

An accounting system earns its keep in the bad week, not the good one. These are the parts nobody asks about until they need them.

  • Nothing is edited awayA posted entry is immutable. Corrections are reversals that leave both the original and the reversal on the record — exactly what an auditor expects to see.
  • Dual controlLarge payments and hand-keyed journals require a second approver, at thresholds you set.
  • Continuous self-checkingAfter every posting the software re-proves that each sub-ledger still agrees with its control account, and says so loudly the moment it does not.
  • Backups that are provenScheduled local backups, encrypted off-site copies safe to keep in a cloud folder, and a one-click drill that restores a backup in a scratch copy to prove it works — without touching your live books.
  • It notices a moved ledgerOpen your books on a second computer and it tells you, before two copies quietly drift apart.
